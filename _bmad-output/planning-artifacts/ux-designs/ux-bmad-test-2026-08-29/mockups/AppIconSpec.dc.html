Three things that go wrong
Fixed stroke width. The mark is drawn at 24×24 with a 1.45 stroke. Exporting at 512 without scaling the stroke gives a hairline that disappears.
Shipping the full mark small. The launcher and tray need the simplified drawing. Two source files, not one scaled.
Strokes left unconverted. Outline them before export so no renderer re-interprets the join and cap.
