- (decision) Exact-alarm and battery-optimisation permissions requested together at first Medication Schedule creation, behind one explanation screen. Play-eligible via REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS since Doze demonstrably breaks the core function
- (decision) Layer depth: three layers (presentation / domain / data), not four. Use-case classes only for genuinely multi-step operations; CRUD goes straight through the repository. Chosen for a solo maintainer across 62 FRs
- (decision) Riverpod without codegen, per Riverpod's own current guidance and the cancellation of Dart macros

- (decision) AD-3 rewritten: DEK is double-wrapped, once by a Keystore-held KEK (fast path) and once by a recovery-phrase-derived KEK, both wrapped copies stored beside the DB.
